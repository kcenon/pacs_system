Detailed Description

Result of parsing an inbound multipart/related MTOM response.

root_xml is the SOAP envelope that constitutes the root part (the one whose Content-ID matches the start parameter or, when absent, the first part). parts is every attachment, keyed on the bare content-id with the angle brackets stripped.
